## Module 8: Education Counselor Assignment & Feedback

Objective: Track counseling quality and session data.

Auto assignment of Education Counselor by using few algorithm and given all options and rating of educational counselor to student – need screen details and selection criteria for auto action.

### 

### **Smart Appointment Scheduling**

Auto-Assignment Algorithm:

Match counselor based on:

1\. Specialization match (student's field ↔ counselor expertise) or student can choose in final

2\. Country expertise (student's target countries ↔ counselor regions)

3\. Availability overlap (student time zone ↔ counselor hours) – Indicate the time zone for information before accepting. Always keep in time limits of counselor and give any time to student if they accept or want to check other.

4\. Workload balancing (distribute evenly across counselors) – Manage schedule of each counselor and no appointment be set on same day.

5\. Past ratings (prefer higher-rated counselors)

Example:

Student: Indian wants Computer Science in USA, Student need to be flexible for time.

Calendar Integration:

- Sync with Google/Outlook calendars

- Automated reminders: 24 hours before, 2 hours before, 10 minutes before

- One-click reschedule option – at least 24 hour before the appointment

- Buffer time between sessions
